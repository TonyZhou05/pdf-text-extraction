• Safetywasdocumented,althoughdoseescalationwaslimitedbythenumberofsubcutaneousinjections,thepotential
for skin toxicity (cellulitis), and low-level anticoagulant effect. Nonetheless, the hypothesis of targeting prothrombotic
pathwaysinpancreasadenocarcinomaremainscompelling.
