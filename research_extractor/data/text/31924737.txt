Supplementary Methods
Planned dose and dose modifications of trifluridine/tipiracil (FTD/TPI)
The planned cumulative dose was based on 5 dosing days for each cycle and the
number of tablets to be administered was based on each patient’s body surface area to
achieve the assigned dose level in mg/m2. Dose intensity in the expansion phase was
calculated by dividing the actual cumulative dose over the cumulative number of weeks
at the end of the cycle. A maximum of three dose reductions of study medication were
permitted in 5-mg/m2 steps to a minimum dose of 20 mg/m2 (40 mg/m2/day). If toxicities
recurred following dose reductions to 20 mg/m2, treatment was discontinued. Dose
escalation was not permitted at any time.
Supplementary Results
Safety – dose-escalation phase
In the dose-escalation phase, (n = 26), all-cause adverse events (AEs) of any grade
occurred in all patients, and grade ≥3 AEs occurred in 69% of patients. Treatment-
