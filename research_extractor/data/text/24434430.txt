Background: Panobinostat, a pan-deacetylase inhibitor, overcomes imatinib resistance in preclinical models of gastrointestinal
stromal tumours (GIST). Here we determined the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) and dose-limiting toxicities (DLT)
of panobinostat incombination with imatinib(IM)for treatment of patientswithrefractory GIST.
Methods: Following a 7-day run-in phase of IM (400mg per day), escalating doses of panobinostat were added following a
‘3plus3’design. Twelveheavily pretreated GISTpatientswere enrolled intwo doselevels.
