Additional Methods
Study design and MTD determination: Patients who received at least 1 dose of
glasdegib and of the co-administered chemotherapeutics were evaluable for the
maximum tolerated dose (MTD) assessment by a standard 3+3 study design. Initially, 3
to 6 patients were treated in each treatment arm with glasdegib starting dose of 100 mg
based on the observed safety, efficacy, pharmacodynamic, and pharmacokinetic data
from the completed phase 1 study in patients with myeloid malignancies by Martinelli et
al.1 If no DLT was observed in the first 3 patients, then 3 additional patients were
treated at the next higher dose level (glasdegib 200 mg). If a DLT was observed in 1 of
the initial 3 treated patients, 3 additional patients were enrolled and treated at the same
dose level. If no further DLT was observed (n = 1 of 6), the dose escalation continued to
the next dose level (glasdegib 200 mg). Dose escalation continued until DLTs were
observed in at least 2 of the patients treated at a dose level, leading to the conclusion
that the MTD has been exceeded and the next lower dose of glasdegib was expanded
to up to 6 patients. If 0 or 1 of 6 patients experienced a DLT, this dose level was
declared the MTD. Once the MTD was declared, 10 subsequent patients entered a
safety expansion component in arms A and C.
Reference
1. Martinelli G, Oehler VG, Papayannidis C, Courtney R, Shaik MN, Zhang X, et al.
Treatment with PF-04449913, an oral smoothened antagonist, in patients with myeloid
malignancies: a phase 1 safety and pharmacokinetics study. Lancet Haematol.
2015;2(8):e339-346.

included B-cell activating factor (BAFF), brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), CA-

necrosis (TNF)-α, TNF-β, and vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF).
