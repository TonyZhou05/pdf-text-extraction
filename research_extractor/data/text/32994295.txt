1. Supplementary Methods
Dose-limiting toxicity
DLT was evaluated during the 9-week treatment period, in DLT evaluable patients which included all
patients who completed 3 cycles of treatment or discontinued study treatment earlier due to a DLT
and received at least 30% of the planned Debio 1143 dose, at least 100 mg/m² of the planned cisplatin
dose, and at least 60% of the planned radiotherapy dose. Patients violating clinically relevant
inclusion/non-inclusion criteria or who received non-permitted concomitant treatments during the
DLT period with a probability of high DLT incidence were excluded.
DLTs and determination of the recommended dose were assessed by an independent efficacy and
safety evaluation committee.
Dose-limiting toxicity is defined using Common Toxicity Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v4.03, as:
• Grade 4 neutropenia without fever >38.5°C, lasting ≥7 days;
• Grade 3 or 4 neutropenia with concomitant fever >38.5°C or grade ≥3 infection;
• Thrombocytopenia <25000/μL lasting ≥5 days or <50000/μL, if bleeding or requiring platelets
transfusion;
• Grade ≥3 non-haematological toxicity (except mucositis and skin reactions and nausea,
vomiting, or diarrhoea unless persisting despite optimal symptomatic therapy);
• Grade 4 skin or mucosal reactions;
• Grade ≥3 decrease in LVEF;
• Grade ≥2 ototoxicity;
• Grade ≥2 worsening of renal function;
• Cisplatin or Debio 1143 treatment delay >2 weeks for related AE;
• Any other life-threatening toxicity.
Efficacy
Patients were considered evaluable for efficacy if they had measurable disease according to RECIST
v1.1, and underwent a baseline disease assessment and at least one post-baseline assessment, and
received ≥70% of the planned doses of either Debio 1143 or radiotherapy and ≥100 mg/m² of the
planned cisplatin dose, and did not have any violations of clinically-relevant eligibility criteria or receive
non-permitted concomitant treatments. Response (per investigator) was evaluated at screening, 10 to
12 weeks after end of treatment, and then every 3 months for 2 years from the initiation of treatment.
Overall response rate is reported per RECIST.
All time-to-event analyses were estimated from Kaplan-Meier plots.
PFS was measured from the start of treatment, with censoring at the last evidence of non-progression
in the event of: a) no death or progression before the end of the observation period, b) initiation of a
prohibited medication or alternative new anti-cancer therapy without progression, c) progression or
death occurring after >15 weeks during which the patient’s progression status was not evaluated (one
patient impacted, with 257 days between last assessment and death).
PFS at 12, 18 and 24 months after EOT, and corresponding 95% CIs were estimated by dose and overall.

OS was measured from the start of treatment, with deaths from any cause considered as events.
Censoring was applied at the last follow-up for patients still alive at the end of the observation period.
OS at 12 and 24 months after EOT, and corresponding 95% CIs were estimated by dose and overall.
Disease-specific survival was measured from the start of treatment, with deaths due to SCCHN
considered as events. Censoring was applied at the last follow-up for patients still alive at the end of
the observation period. Censoring at the date of death was applied for patients who died from causes
other than SCCHN.
Locoregional control (LRC) was censored at the time of any of the following: a) death without LRC loss,
b) no locoregional failure before the end of the observation period, c) no locoregional failure prior to
initiating a prohibited medication or alternative new anti-cancer therapy, d) locoregional failure after
>15 weeks during which the patient’s LRC status was not evaluated.
LRC and distant relapse rates at 6, 12, and 18 months after EOT, and corresponding 95% CIs were
estimated.
