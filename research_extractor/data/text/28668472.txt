Organ and Marrow function requirements for inclusion:
Leukocytes 3,000/mcL,
Neutrophils 1,500/mcL,
Platelets 100,000/mcL,
Hemoglobin >10 g/dL,
Total bilirubin within institutional upper limit of normal (ULN),
AST/ALT 5 times ULN,
Creatinine ULN or creatinine clearance 45mL/min/1.73 m2 if creatinine >ULN,
PT-INR/PTT <1.5 times ULN*
(cid:950)
* Patients receiving therapeutic anti-coagulation were permitted provided there
was no prior PT-INR/PTT abnormality.
