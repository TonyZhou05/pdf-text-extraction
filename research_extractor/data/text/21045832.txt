BACKGROUND:Thecombinationofsorafenib(vascularendothelialgrowthfactorreceptor2inhibitor)andsirolimus(mammaliantarget
(cid:2) of rapamycin inhibitor) might work synergistically.
(cid:2)
(cid:2)

METHODS: A phase I dose-escalation study with sorafenib twice a day (b.i.d.) and sirolimus once daily (q.d.) was performed to
(cid:2) determinetherecommendeddoseofthecombinationinpatientswithsolidtumours.Secondaryobjectivesweretodeterminethe
(cid:2)
(cid:2) (cid:2) safety profile and maximum tolerated dose (MTD), and to evaluate the pharmacokinetics (PK) of the combination.
(cid:2) (cid:2) RESULTS: Dose-limiting toxicities were transaminitis and cutaneous toxicity. The most frequently reported adverse events were
(cid:2)
(cid:2) elevated transaminases, hypophosphatemia, fatigue, anorexia, diarrhoea, nausea, rash and palmar–plantar erythrodysaesthesia.
(cid:2)
(cid:2) Sirolimus did not change the PK of sorafenib; in contrast, sorafenib reduced the AUC (0(cid:2)96) and C max of sirolimus. No objective
(cid:2)
(cid:2) responses were observed; eight patients showed stable disease for a median of 16.3 weeks (range 8–24). The MTD of the
(cid:2)
(cid:2) combination was sorafenib 200mg b.i.d. with sirolimus 1mg q.d.
(cid:2)
(cid:2) CONCLUSION:Thecombinationofsorafenibandsirolimusshowedenhancedtoxicity,whichcouldnotbeexplainedbythePKofboth
(cid:2)
(cid:2) drugs. The relative low doses at the MTD, in combination with the PK results, do not warrant further development of this
(cid:2)
(cid:2) combination.
