Phase I Study of a Combination of Irinotecan and Ifosfamide
in Advanced Primary Lung Cancer
MASAO ICHIKI, TORU RIKIMARU, YO OKUBO, RUMI GOHARA, TAKASHI MITUI ,
TOMOTAKA KAWAYAMA, MASAHARU KINOSHITA
AND KOTARO OIZUMI
Department of Medicine, Kurume University School of Medicine,
Kurume 830-0011, Japan
Summary: We sought to investigate cisplatin-free chemotherapy for patients with primary lung
cancer. We therefore conducted a Phase II study to identify; a) the maximum tolerated dose (MTD)
of irinotecan (CPT-11) given with a fixed dose of ifosfamide (IFM), and b) the principal toxic effects
associated with this regimen in a Phase I study. A total of 27 patients with previously treated or
untreated primary lung cancer received OPT-11 on days 1, 8 and 15 in combination with a fixed
dose of IFM, 1.5 g/m2/day, on days 1 through 3, given every 4 weeks. The starting dose of OPT-11
was 30 mg/m2, which was increased by amounts of 10 mg/m2. Four patients were assigned to
different dosage levels, and drug toxicity was evaluated for the first 2 cycles. The MTD of OPT-11
was 90 mg/m2, with leukopenia being the dose-limiting effect. The response rate was 43% (6114;1
complete response) in non-small cell lung cancer, and 78% (7/9; no complete response) in small
cell lung cancer. The recommended dose of OPT-11 for a Phase II study is thus 80 mg/m2 on days
1, 8 and 15 with IFM 1.5 g/m2 given on days 1 through 3. This regimen appears particularly
encouraging, because of its low toxicity. Phase II trials of the combination are indicated.
